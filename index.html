Member Upgrade Open House

Your membership, wide open.

For eight days, every Play and 1Fit member gets full access to all CA Fit&Play amenities. Nothing to sign up for, no appointment, nothing added to your account. Just check in the way you always do.

Monday August 24 through Monday August 31:

  • Every facility. Three fitness clubs, the pools, tennis and pickleball courts, the ice rink and Columbia SportsPark.
  • Every day of the window, during each facility's normal operating hours.
  • No signup and no appointment. Open the CA app and check in as usual.
  • Upgrade by August 31 and you are treated as a new annual member, with no enrollment fee.

One limit worth knowing. Open access covers the facilities themselves. It does not carry the booking and reservation privileges of a Fit&Play membership. If you want a class, a court time or a lane that needs a reservation, ask a membership advocate and they will arrange it for you.

The details

  • Who this is for. Current CA Play and CA 1Fit members with an active membership in good standing. This is not a public offer.
  • Access window. Monday August 24 through Monday August 31, during each facility's normal operating hours.
  • Reservations. Open house access does not include booking privileges. Reservations are arranged manually by a membership advocate.
  • Upgrade deadline. Upgrades must be completed by close of business Monday August 31.
  • Promotional benefits. The comped first month, the waived August prorate, the free year of KidSpace, the free JumpStart session and eligibility for the $99 Value-Added Package are available on new annual CA Fit&Play memberships only.
  • Already in a contract. If you are part way through a current membership term, you can still upgrade. A membership advocate will walk you through it.
  • Golf. Golf memberships are not included in this open house.
  • Facility notes. The Women's Gym at Columbia Gym is closed for renovation from August 21. Equipment is being rotated at all three fitness clubs during the week of August 24. The lower parking lot at Supreme Sports Club is closed August 31.
