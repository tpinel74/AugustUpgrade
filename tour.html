# Member Upgrade Open House - Email and SMS Sequence ### August 24 to 31, 2026 | CA Play and CA 1Fit segments --- ## Sequence strategy **Type:** Upgrade / upsell, compressed into an 8-day event window. **Goal:** Two conversions, in this order. First, get members to physically check in at a facility their tier does not normally cover. Second, convert that experience into an annual CA Fit&Play upgrade by close of business August 31. **Why the order matters.** The check-in is the conversion that makes the upgrade possible. A member who never walks in has nothing to decide on August 31. So the first half of the sequence sells the visit and only mentions the offer; the second half sells the offer to people who have already had the experience. **Narrative arc** | Phase | Days | Emotional register | Primary ask | |---|---|---|---| | Open | Mon 24, Tue 25, Wed 26 | Curious, permissive, low-effort | Come use it | | Turn | Fri 28 | Enthusiastic, weekend-facing | Come use it, and here is what it costs to keep | | Close | Sun 30, Mon 31 | Direct, deadline-led | Decide today | **Segmentation.** Play and 1Fit members are unlocking almost opposite things, so every send is written twice. Play members gain the indoor half of CA. 1Fit members gain everything outdoors plus the two clubs that are not their home club. Never send a Play member a message about outdoor pools they already have. **Voice.** Plain, warm, second person. No exclamation marks in subject lines. Spaced hyphens throughout, no em dashes. --- ## Send calendar | # | Date | Day | Play angle | 1Fit angle | SMS? | |---|---|---|---|---|---| | 1 | Mon Aug 24 | Day 1 | The doors are open | The doors are open | Yes | | 2 | Tue Aug 25 | Day 2 | Walk onto the floor | Swim outside | | | 3 | Wed Aug 26 | Day 3 | The class schedule | Courts and the rink | Yes | | 4 | Fri Aug 28 | Day 5 | Try a different club | The family weekend | Yes | | 5 | Sun Aug 30 | Day 7 | Two days left | Two days left | | | 6 | Mon Aug 31 | Day 8 | Last day | Last day | Yes | SMS recommended on four of the six days. Segment at the point of send, not once at the start, so that anyone who upgrades mid-window is handled correctly. **Exit conditions** - **Upgraded to annual CA Fit&Play** → exit immediately, all channels. This is the single most important rule in the sequence. A member who upgrades on Tuesday and then receives four more "upgrade by August 31" emails will read it as CA not knowing who they are. - **Unsubscribed** → exit, per standard handling. - **SMS opt-out** → exit SMS only, remain on email. **Branching** - **Checked in out of tier at any point** → move to the short track. They have had the experience, so they skip the remaining "come try it" sends and receive only Fri 28, Sun 30 and Mon 31, where the offer is the message. Reduces their touch count from six to three. - **No opens across emails 1 through 3** → suppress the Sunday send. Somebody who has ignored four emails does not need a fifth before the final one. Send Friday and Monday only. - **Opened but never clicked** → no change. The click is not the conversion here. A member can read the email, walk into a club, and never touch the landing page. **Suppression** - Anyone already enrolled in a separate active Club Automation campaign for the same window. - Anyone with an open service or billing issue, at June's discretion. - Golf-only members. Golf is out of scope for this open house. **Timing.** Morning sends, roughly 6 to 7am, so the message lands before the day is planned. Friday and Sunday are the exceptions worth testing at late afternoon, since both are setting up the following day rather than the current one. --- # What to measure The click-through rate is the least important number in this campaign. Track it, but do not judge on it. | Metric | Why it matters | |---|---| | **Out-of-tier check-ins, by member** | The actual conversion. Pull daily during the window, not just on September 1. | | **Unique members who checked in at least once** | Reach of the experience. Denominator for everything below. | | **Upgrades closed, by segment** | Split Play and 1Fit. They will not convert at the same rate. | | **Annualized dues added** | The number that matters. Unit counts alone understate a Fit&Play-weighted result. | | **Participant conversion rate** | Share of check-in participants who upgraded. Tells you whether to run this again. | | **Incremental upgrades** | Against the roughly 14-per-month baseline. | | **Unsubscribe and SMS opt-out rate** | The cost of the frequency. If this exceeds 1% on either channel, the cadence was too heavy. | Daily check-in pulls matter more than they look. If Monday and Tuesday come in flat, there is still time to change Wednesday's subject line or add a send. If you wait until September 1 to look, the campaign is over. --- # Open items before build 1. **Landing page URL and UTM parameters** for all email and SMS links, per the canonical taxonomy. 2. **The main membership number** appears in five emails as [main number].
